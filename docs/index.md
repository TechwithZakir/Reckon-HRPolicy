# Attendance Policy and Payroll Automation for Frappe HR: Late, Early Exit, Break and Overtime Rules

In many Bangladeshi companies, attendance rules such as late-arrival penalties, break limits and overtime blocks are applied by hand at payroll time. **Reckon HR Policy** by Reckon Technologies Ltd. turns those rules into configurable settings on Frappe HR v16, so the native Salary Slip carries the result with full evidence.

## Detailed features

### Attendance consequences

- Late arrival policy: grace minutes and a number of late days that equal one deduction day
- Early exit policy with its own grace and threshold
- Break policy: maximum break minutes, with violations converted to deduction days
- Sundays and native holidays excluded from policy events

### Payroll additions

- Daily allowance per payment day for monthly employees
- Early-entry overtime paid in complete blocks per shift
- Four selectable day-rate bases: working days, payment days, calendar days or gross
- Reserved Salary Components that cannot be duplicated by Additional Salary

### Employee types

- Monthly and hourly payroll types, with dated assignment rates
- No Attendance Deduction policy for exempt employees
- Hourly employees paid from approved native Timesheets

### Shifts and calendars

- Managed Regular and Saturday shifts with native auto-attendance
- Holiday lists for the current and next year
- Saturday shift assignments extended automatically ahead of time

### Automatic checkout

- Hourly job that closes open check-ins after a configurable number of hours
- Synthetic checkout never counts as paid hours or overtime

### Audit and reporting

- Immutable attendance summary stored with every submitted Salary Slip, with a SHA-256 digest
- Policy effectiveness report with employee-wise applicability, deduction breakdowns, overtime preview and Smart Help
- Reports respect Frappe user permissions

### Controls

- Central Policy Settings with effective date, company account mappings and timezone-safe calculation
- Role-restricted settings and server-side permission checks

## Built on Frappe HR

Reckon HR Policy is an installable Frappe app on top of Frappe HR v16. It uses no core patches, Server Scripts or replacement payroll engine: the native Salary Slip, Attendance, Employee Checkin and Payroll Entry stay in charge.

## Who it is for

HR and payroll teams in garments, manufacturing, retail and services who run shift-based attendance with policy-based deductions and allowances.
